# Copywriting Patterns & Structure

## Homepage Section Order

Follow this proven sequence for maximum conversion and SEO:

1. **Hero Section** - Problem/solution headline + benefit-driven subhead
2. **3-Step Process** - Visual credibility builder (optional but effective)
3. **Lead Form #1** - Early conversion opportunity
4. **Mission/About** - Emotional connection + brand story
5. **UVP/Why Us** - 3-4 benefit blocks with visuals
6. **Trust/Credibility Section** - Deep dive (local roots, reputation, transparency)
7. **CTA + Lead Form #2** - Second conversion point
8. **Problem-Aware Section** - Bottom-of-funnel keyword targeting
9. **Testimonials** - Social proof with real names/initials
10. **Areas We Serve** - Local SEO goldmine (counties → cities → neighborhoods)
11. **Email Signup** - List building opportunity
12. **Footer** - Contact info, hours, social links

## Writing Style Guidelines

### Tone & Voice
- Direct, benefit-driven language
- "You" language throughout (not "we")
- Conversational but professional
- Zero fluff, all substance
- Problem → Solution framework

### Formatting Rules
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines maximum)
- Bullet points for keyword-rich lists
- Bold for key benefits and numbers
- Specific numbers over vague claims (e.g., "7-14 days" not "fast")
- H2 headers for main sections, H3 for subsections

### Persuasion Elements
- Lead with benefit, not feature
- Use specific numbers ($0 fees, 7-14 days, 100% local)
- Address objections directly
- Include urgency without pressure
- Emphasize "no risk" elements

## Section Templates

### Hero Formula
**Headline:** [Action Verb] + [Primary Benefit] + [Time/Speed Element]
- Example: "Sell Your House Fast. Cash in Days, Not Months."

**Subhead:** [What You Do] + [Key Differentiators] + [Who You Serve]
- Example: "We buy houses in any condition—no agents, no fees, no repairs. Get a fair cash offer from local Pittsburgh buyers who actually close."

### Mission/About Formula
**Header:** [Aspirational Statement]
- Example: "Redefining the Standard of Fast Real Estate Sales"

**Body Structure:**
- What you do (concrete)
- Why you do it (mission/values)
- Who you serve (target audience)
- CTA to action

**Length:** 3-4 short paragraphs max

### Why Us / UVP Formula
Create 3-4 benefit blocks, each with:
- **Icon/Image** (visual anchor)
- **Benefit Header** (3-6 words, benefit-focused)
- **2-3 Paragraphs** explaining the benefit
- **Key differentiator** in bold

**Pattern:**
1. Address main pain point (condition/repairs)
2. Speed/timeline benefit
3. Cost/transparency benefit
4. Optional: Process/experience benefit

### Trust/Credibility Formula
**Header:** "Why Trust [Brand Name]?"

**3-Block Structure:**
1. **Local Proof** - "Deep Local Roots", neighborhood knowledge, community commitment
2. **Performance Proof** - "A Reputation for Results", guarantees, track record
3. **Process Proof** - "Transparent & Direct", simplified process, zero hidden costs

Each block: H3 header + bold key phrase + 2-3 paragraphs

### Problem-Aware Section Formula
**Purpose:** Capture bottom-of-funnel searches

**Header:** "For [Target Audience] with [Specific Problem]"
- Example: "For Homeowners with 'Unsellable' Properties"

**Subhead:** [Restate problem with empathy]

**Body Structure:**
- Acknowledge the challenge
- "If you're searching for:" [bullet list of actual search queries]
- "You've found the right [solution]"
- Restate core benefits
- CTA

**Critical:** Include 4-6 actual keyword variations people search for

### Testimonial Formula
**Structure per testimonial:**
- 2-3 sentence quote focusing on results/outcome
- Include specific detail (timeline, amount saved, key benefit)
- Attribution: "- [First Initial]. [Last Name]" or "- [First Name] [Last Initial]."

**Placement:** 3-5 testimonials, real people only

### Areas We Serve Formula
**Structure:**
- H2: "Counties, Cities & Areas We Serve"
- Subhead: "A small sample of the areas in our buy box."
- Group by County (H3)
- Bulleted list of cities/neighborhoods under each county
- 6-10 locations per county

**SEO Benefit:** Each location becomes a rankable keyword combination

## Keyword Integration Rules

### Primary Keywords
- Place in H1 (hero headline)
- Place in meta title and description
- Use 2-3 times naturally in first 200 words
- Include in at least one H2

### Secondary Keywords
- Distribute across H2/H3 headers
- Include in benefit blocks
- Use in problem-aware section list
- Feature in areas served list

### Local Keywords
- City + Service in hero
- Neighborhood names in areas served
- County references in trust section
- "[Service] near me" variations in problem-aware section

### LSI Keywords
- Related terms throughout body copy
- Industry-specific vocabulary
- Problem-solution phrases
- Alternative ways people describe the service

## Length Guidelines

- **Hero:** 15-25 words headline, 30-50 words subhead
- **Mission/About:** 80-120 words total
- **Why Us blocks:** 60-80 words each
- **Trust section:** 200-250 words total
- **Problem-Aware:** 120-150 words
- **Total homepage:** 800-1,200 words optimal
